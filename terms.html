Terms.

Every Ixtla rug is made to order. When you place an order, Edgardo begins weaving it by hand — nothing is pre-made or held in stock, so an order can't be changed or cancelled once weaving has started.

Each rug is one of one. Small variations in colour, weave and fringe come from natural dye and handwork — they're part of the rug, not a defect.

Prices are shown in USD and include worldwide shipping. Payment is processed securely by Stripe; Ixtla never sees or stores your card details.

See Shipping & handling for delivery timing and our return policy.

Questions about an order? Write to hola@ixtla.com.
