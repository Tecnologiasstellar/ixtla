Privacy.

To make and ship your rug, we collect your name, email, phone number and shipping address at checkout, through Stripe. That's all we ask for.

We use it only to fulfil your order and to contact you about it — never to sell to, or share with, anyone beyond the people who need it to get your rug to you: Stripe, who processes payment, and the courier who delivers it.

This site runs no analytics, trackers or advertising cookies. Stripe's checkout page sets its own cookies to process payment securely, governed by Stripe's privacy policy.

We keep order records as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. Write to hola@ixtla.com any time to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.
